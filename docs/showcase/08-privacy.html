A companion, not a conversation warehouse

Your sessions stay with their original tools.

Native files are the source of truth. Cookbench keeps only the minimum metadata needed to show and return to a Stove.

Cookbench keeps

  • Bounded presentation metadata
  • Pin and Archive state
  • Minimum verified locators
  • Notification preferences

Cookbench does not keep

  • Complete conversations
  • Prompt or tool content
  • SQLite transcript database
  • Telemetry or inbound messages
Observe the work. Do not take ownership of it.
