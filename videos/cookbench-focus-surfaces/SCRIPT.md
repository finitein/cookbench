# Cookbench Focus Surfaces - Narration And Burned-in Captions

Voice direction: natural, restrained Mandarin product narration in the Dylan
voice, generated with `Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-CustomVoice`. Delivery is about
ten percent quicker than a conventional technology voiceover, with clear words,
short breaths, and no sales cadence. The 22.40-second delivery starts at 0.20s.

> Agent 一多，我就得来回切终端看进度。Cookbench 把状态放到桌面，极简模式只留最重要的 Stove。拖到顶边，空闲时自动收起，鼠标触顶再出现。运行、等待、完成，一眼分清。它不复制完整对话，也不接管 Agent。看清状态，继续专注。

The narration intentionally describes visible lifecycle states without claiming
that the currently suspended dynamic macOS status-bar Stove runtime is active.
There is no background music.

Burned-in captions:

1. `又要切回终端？ 状态散在每个窗口里。`
2. `少一点干扰，多一点专注。`
3. `顶边停靠。需要时出现，空闲时收起。`
4. `macOS 状态 Stoves：烹饪中、等待处理、已完成。`
5. `原工具仍是工作现场。Cookbench 只观察必要状态，不复制完整对话，也不接管 Agent。`
6. `Cookbench。看清状态，继续专注。`
